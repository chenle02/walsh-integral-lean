Reusable declaration index

IsWhiteNoise is a primitive compatibility interface, with an explicit completed Gaussian-product existence theorem. walshT is zero outside its predictable finite-variance domain; that totalization is not evidence of integrability. Finite mixed norm for p ≥ 2 on a probability space implies the genuine L² domain. CausalKernel assumes deterministic Borel causality, L² section membership and L² continuity on the whole real output plane. Predictable-version equality is separate almost-sure equality at each output.
