Mathematical sources

The formalization concerns classical Gaussian white noise and Walsh integration. The martingale-difference estimate follows E. Rio, Moment Inequalities for Sums of Dependent Random Variables under Projective Conditions, Journal of Theoretical Probability 22 (2009), 146–163; primary reference DOI. General foundations come from Mathlib.
